# Curriculum comparison for the 100-hour learning plan

Reviewed 2026-10-03 for junior LLM application jobs in India and India-eligible remote work, using free access and one document Q&A lab. The [market review](learning-plan-market-review-2026-10-02.md) supplies the dated job evidence. This document records which curriculum material earns time in the plan.

## Coverage and method

Compared all 523 English phase lessons across 20 phases and all 67 certification lessons: 590 distinct source pages. The comparison used titles, objectives where present, prerequisites, time estimates and topic structure. Legacy lessons without a standard objectives section were checked through their problem/concept overview. Subagents reviewed separate phase ranges. The orchestrator reviewed certifications, checked proposed additions and integrated the decisions.

This is a relevance and coverage review. Candidate sections and code received closer checks where they could change the plan; the review did not execute or audit all 590 implementations. The selected 19.68 demo and its 24 existing deterministic tests passed. These review checks do not count as learner work. Saved progress remains at slot 1 with zero minutes used.

The schedule now links 38 distinct lesson pages, up from 32. Five additions supply focused sections or checks inside existing hours. The sixth replaces an optional hour with a metric exercise. The budget stays at 100 hours.

## Priorities and cuts

The core route retains real generation, learned embeddings and model-selected tool use; ingestion and retrieval; schemas, citations and abstention; access and error checks; versioned evaluation; local FastAPI; Chroma persistence; LCEL comparison; and bounded LangGraph. The additions use the same lab and approved dependencies.

Full reranking moves to reference. Hybrid retrieval in slot 38 and local rollback in slot 69 remain optional and can fund repair first. Seventeen low-priority entries were removed from the reference shelf; the full ledger keeps their rationale. Broad platform surveys, multimodal/voice work, fine-tuning and self-hosting, research systems, multi-agent coordination, deep MCP/OAuth, production scaling and certification routes stay outside the required allocation. They can be reconsidered if the job target changes.

The learner allowed up to four mock hours to become essential lessons if needed. This review leaves that allowance unused: 100 hours, 12 mocks, 12 recall/repair slots and two application-material hours remain. Added checks share existing 45-minute work blocks. Slot 9 uses a compact case manifest, and later tuning/comparisons use T cases after H results have been inspected. Unfinished work follows the recorded repair and reallocation rules.
